SECTION 1: REFUND & RETURN POLICY
-----------------------------------
We offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee on all purchases. If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.

How to get a refund:
- Contact our support team with your order ID and the reason for your refund request.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to your original payment method.
- For subscription plans, refunds are prorated based on the unused portion of your billing cycle.
- Digital products and one-time setup fees are non-refundable after 30 days.
- If your account was charged in error, we will issue a full refund immediately upon verification.

Can I get a refund? Yes, absolutely. If you are within the 30-day window, simply provide your order ID and we will process your refund without any questions asked.

SECTION 2: BILLING & PAYMENTS
-------------------------------
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans.

Billing cycle options:
- Monthly billing: Charged on the same date every month.
- Annual billing: Charged once per year at a discounted rate (save 20%).
- Invoices are sent automatically via email on each billing date.

If you see an unexpected charge:
- Log into your account and check the Billing section for a full breakdown.
- Common reasons include plan upgrades, overage charges, or renewal fees.
- If a charge is not recognized, contact us within 60 days and we will investigate immediately.
- Disputed charges are resolved within 3-5 business days.

How to cancel your subscription:
- Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription.
- Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
- We do not charge cancellation fees.

SECTION 4: ACCOUNT & LOGIN ISSUES
------------------------------------
Forgot your password:
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address.
- A password reset link will be sent to you within 2 minutes.
- If you do not receive the email, check your spam/junk folder.
- Password reset links expire after 24 hours.

Account locked:
- Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts for security.
- Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the "Forgot Password" link to reset.
- Contact support if your account is permanently locked.

Changing your email or account details:
- Go to Settings > Profile to update your name, email, or phone number.
- Email changes require verification of the new email address.
- For security reasons, password changes require confirmation of your current password.

Two-factor authentication (2FA):
- We strongly recommend enabling 2FA in Settings > Security.
- Supported methods: SMS, authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy).
- If you lose access to your 2FA device, contact support with your account ID for manual verification.

SECTION 5: TECHNICAL SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------
Common issues and solutions:

App not loading or showing a blank screen:
- Clear your browser cache and cookies, then refresh the page.
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge are fully supported).
- Disable browser extensions, especially ad blockers, which may interfere.
- Check our status page at status.helpdeskai.com for any ongoing incidents.

Slow performance:
- Ensure your internet connection is stable (minimum 5 Mbps recommended).
- Close unused browser tabs to free up memory.
- Contact support if the issue persists for more than 30 minutes.

Error messages:
- If you see a 500 Internal Server Error, this is on our end. Our team is automatically notified and will fix it within 2 hours.
- If you see a 403 Forbidden error, your account may lack the required permissions. Contact your administrator.
- For any other error, take a screenshot and send it to support@helpdeskai.com with a description of what you were doing.

Data not saving:
- Ensure you are clicking the Save button after making changes.
- Check if you have a stable internet connection.
- If data was lost due to a system error, contact us immediately. We retain backups for 30 days.

SECTION 7: PRIVACY & DATA SECURITY
-------------------------------------
We take data security very seriously. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

Data retention:
- Active account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- After account deletion, data is permanently purged within 30 days.

GDPR compliance:
- We are fully GDPR compliant for customers in the European Union.
- You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time.
- Submit a Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) via our privacy portal or email privacy@helpdeskai.com.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We use data only to provide and improve our service.

SECTION 10: CANCELLATION & ACCOUNT DELETION
----------------------------------------------
Cancelling your subscription:
- Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
- You will not be charged further after cancellation.
- Your data remains accessible until the end of the billing period.

Deleting your account:
- Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
- All data associated with your account will be deleted within 30 days.
- Make sure to export any important data before deleting.

Reactivating a cancelled account:
- You can reactivate your account within 30 days of cancellation by logging in and choosing a plan.
- After 30 days, you will need to create a new account.
